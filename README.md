  <ins> **Abstract:** </ins>
   Our project ‘WAREHOUSE INVENTORY AND REORDER SYSTEM’ is designed to make the management of Stock levels, product tracking, and automated restocking within the warehouse environment.
This project focuses on solving common challenges such as inaccurate inventory records, delays in restocking, and improves efficiency in comparison to manual processes. By integrating a database oriented approach with a GUI, the system enables Real-Time monitoring of product sales, Quantities, categorization of items, and generation of reorder alerts when stock falls below the predefined level ensuring optimal inventory levels, reducing the risk of stockouts or overstocking, and promotes overall operational efficiency.
The GUI also includes reporting features that provide insights into product movement, supplier performance, and demand trends, thereby assisting managers in making data driven decisions. Ultimately, this project demonstrates how automation and structured inventory control can improve warehouse productivity, minimize costs, and support sustainable supply chain management.
